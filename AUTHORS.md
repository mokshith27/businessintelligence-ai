## Team agreement

This is a **three-member team**. Instead of assigning per-member effort percentages, the
team shares responsibility across a role map that naturally separates the work:

- **Core analytical engine & data science** — ingestion, KPI construction, materiality
  and event analysis, driver decomposition, evidence fusion, confidence and abstention,
  actions, personas, LLM narratives, causal analysis, scenarios, API, and dashboard.
- **Documentation, community & repository organization** — quickstart and architecture
  guides, the contributing guide, community health files, the changelog, and release
  notes.
- **Review, validation & quality** — cross-checking every change, running the controlled
  scenario and sparse-history validations, and keeping output audits reproducible.

Git history shows who changed what, and `AUTHORS.md` records who owns responsibility for
each area.

## How to update this file

When a new contributor joins, add a row (role, GitHub, and contact email) after the
whole team agrees. AUTHORS is not used to compute share or effort; it documents roles.

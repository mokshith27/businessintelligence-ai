## Contribution split

The team agreed to a **65% / 35%** effort split:

- **Mokshith (65%)** — core engine design and implementation: ingestion, KPI
  construction, materiality and event analysis, driver decomposition, evidence
  fusion, confidence and abstention, actions, personas, LLM narratives, causal
  analysis, controlled scenarios, API, dashboard, and the runtime pipeline.
- **Mukesh (35%)** — project documentation (quickstart and architecture guides),
  the contributing guide, repository community health files, changelog and release
  notes, and overall repository organization supporting collaboration.

The split reflects the team agreement; git history shows who changed what, and
`AUTHORS.md` records who owns responsibility for each area.
